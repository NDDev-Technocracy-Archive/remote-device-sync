# Remote quality checkpoint

The agreed product is standalone NDDev Remote, ready for embedding without an
NDS account, telemetry or synchronization requirement. Native viewing and
control cover Ubuntu x64 X11/Wayland and Apple Silicon Macs. The requested
clipboard scope is text, images and files in both directions and across tabs.
The owner requested a checkpoint, recorded remaining issues and session closure.

## Physical evidence and limits

Twenty-four measured static native-viewer intervals across the two Macs had
zero attributed process disk writes and zero diagnostic snapshot/incident
writes. The CPU results were mixed and do not establish a general reduction.
The balanced samples used about one fifth of one CPU core. These short,
synthetic intervals do not qualify native capture, WAN behavior, power,
hardware codecs or input-to-photon latency. Full evidence and unchanged
negative results are in [#214](https://github.com/NDDev-Technocracy-OpenNetwork/nddev-remote/issues/214).

The installed Wayland session reported no clipboard grant despite available
portal interfaces. GNOME restored-permission behavior is a documented candidate,
not a proven reading of the opaque token. No saved permission was reset or
overridden, and no fresh consent dialog was opened during the checkpoint.
The available local renewal command still requires an attended qualification.

Installed agents and host applications retain their previous bytes, identities,
processes and OS consent. The read-only compatibility probes preserved manager
instances, generations, selected sessions and host PIDs. Temporary probe CLIs
were removed. No release tag, installed application, device pin, shared cache,
controller or SDK was changed for this checkpoint.
